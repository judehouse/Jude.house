Not a hype recap. A story you can stand inside again.

Jude House captures genuine weddings as they unravel. That means staying present for the quiet before the ceremony, the crack in a vow, the way a room changes during a toast, and all the in-between moments nobody could stage.

The goal is not to turn your wedding into someone else’s fast-cut highlight reel. It is to tell the story of the people, emotion, and feeling that made the day yours—grounded, authentic, and real.

Built around the actual shape of your day.

Every wedding has its own rhythm. Some begin in a quiet room with letters and friends; others move between a ceremony, a sunset portrait window, a packed dance floor, and a final exit. Coverage is shaped around the parts that matter to you, with room for the real moments that happen when no one is checking a box.
