For couples in the planning stage

A more considered wedding day.

Small choices around light, time, and sound make a wedding film feel much more like the real thing.

Useful planning tools.

Give portraits room to breathe.

When the ceremony, family photos, and couple portraits are packed tightly together, the day can begin to feel like a relay race. A little margin makes room for actual moments.

Protect the words.

Ask about ceremony microphones and a simple speech-audio plan. The most meaningful footage in a wedding film is often something someone said.

Let the schedule reflect the priorities.

If dancing with your people matters, keep the camera there. If letters or a private first look matter, give them an unrushed place in the timeline.
